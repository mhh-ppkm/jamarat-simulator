Jamarat 3D Simulation + Agentic AI

A five-level Jamarat facility modelled in true 3D from the aerial photograph: stacked decks, three oval basins and pillars, white tensile canopies, curved ramps from Mina and towers. Synthetic Sawaher-style indicators feed Agent 1, Agent 1 feeds Agent 2, and a human safety gate approves every route change. A conventional baseline runs on the same demand for comparison.

04 Explanation

Scope and safety

The five decks represent the physical Jamarat levels, not risk levels. Risk labels L1–L4 are artificial demonstration thresholds and are not Saudi operating limits.

Route changes are never automated. Only groups with an explicitly modelled permitted alternative are eligible, and a human must approve the hypothetical change.

Figures represent cohorts, not individual pilgrims

The building is drawn at its actual proportions: five decks, 12 m storey height, 950 m by 80 m. Each figure is a pilgrim in ihram (men in white, women in dark abaya), and every group follows a leader carrying a flag in its level colour. Groups wait at the foot of their ramp on the Mina side, walk the deck, stand around each basin to throw, and leave toward Makkah. From far away the figures are drawn larger so you can still see them; in pilgrim eye view they are life size. A red barrier marks Level 3 while it is restricted.

08 Agent 2

Scheduling recommendations

Monitors the pre-approved hypothetical timetable and proposes a safe hold or an eligible alternative. A supervisor approves or rejects route changes. In the 3D view, Agent 2 is the amber core beside Agent 1, linked to the amber release gate at the foot of each level ramp.

AI execution safeguardsResearch only

AI scheduler active when data is fresh. No external actions.

09 Data and risk governance

Active decision policy

Human-led decision support. No live system integration.
L1: maintain schedule
L2: bounded micro-delay (pre-approved demo rule)
L3: hold and request human approval for any eligible reroute
L4: fail-safe, block new departures; safety command simulated

Agentic logic is deterministic rule-based modelling for reproducible research. It is not a trained Saudi operational AI model.
