# Jamarat Agentic AI 3D Simulator

Research prototype by Ir. Ts. Dr. Muhammad Hafiz HASSAN, School of Mechanical Engineering, Universiti Sains Malaysia.

A 3D simulator of the five-level Jamarat facility in which two supervised AI agents schedule pilgrim groups. Agent 1 forecasts the load on each level 15 minutes ahead; Agent 2 proposes delays and reroutes that a human supervisor must approve. All results use synthetic data; no real Sawaher or Tafweej data is used.

Open the live app: https://mhh-ppkm.github.io/jamarat-simulator/

On a phone, open the link in Chrome (Android) and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". After the first visit the simulator also works offline.
